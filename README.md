# 🔍 TruthLens AI — See Beyond the Headlines

TruthLens AI is an AI-powered misinformation detection and fact-verification platform designed to help people identify fake news, suspicious URLs, manipulated images, and misleading information. It analyzes digital content, verifies factual claims against supporting evidence, and generates credibility scores with explanations and references to reliable sources.

Don't just read the news. Verify it.

# 🚀 Overview

- In a world where misinformation spreads rapidly across social media, websites, and messaging platforms, distinguishing facts from fabricated information has become increasingly difficult.
- TruthLens AI combines artificial intelligence, source analysis, and evidence-based verification to help users make informed decisions about the content they encounter online.
- Users can submit text, news articles, URLs, or images and receive an analysis containing a credibility score, factual findings, supporting evidence, and potential misinformation indicators.
